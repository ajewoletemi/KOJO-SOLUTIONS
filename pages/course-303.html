Exclusive Resource: Business Email Setup

As a Class 303 member you now have access to the full guide on creating professional, inbox-receivable business emails.

1. Get a Domain

Buy a domain that matches your brand (Namecheap, Cloudflare, Google Domains, etc.).

2. Choose a Provider

  • Google Workspace — best overall deliverability
  • Microsoft 365 — excellent for Outlook users
  • Zoho Mail — strong free / affordable option
  • Cloudflare Email Routing — free forwarding

3. Configure DNS (Critical)

Add the records your provider gives you:

  • MX — where email is delivered
  • SPF — who is allowed to send as you
  • DKIM — cryptographic proof of authenticity
  • DMARC — policy for failed authentication

Correct SPF + DKIM + DMARC is what keeps your emails out of spam.

4. Create & Test

Create addresses like info@, support@, or yourname@. Send test emails to Gmail and Outlook. Use mail-tester.com to score your setup.
